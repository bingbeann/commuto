# Commuto

## Problem to solve

> The "why" behind your product or feature, articulating the issues aimed to be solved. This is critical because it 
> ensures that everyone understand the purpose and value of what you're building.

Malaysia is aiming to increase public transit modal share from roughly 19% to 40% by 2030, reducing reliance on private 
vehicles. [^1]

However, there's not many options in the market to allow users efficiently get real-time public transit 
updates without encountering heavy friction, clutter or paywalls. 

For user to start relying on public transit, a few core needs must be fulfilled:

1. **Uncompromising punctuality and predictability**: commuters need to know accurate ETAs and live vehicle location so 
they can plan their trips efficiently.
2. **Low friction information access**: easy to understand transit information without invasive ads
3. **Smart departure guidance**: commuters shouldn't have to constantly check their phones to know when to leave their home
or office to catch a bus or train.
4. **Cross operator integration**: unified tracking across Rapid KL (LRT, MRT, Monorail, Feeder Bus), KTMB and regional bus
services under a single roof.

Commuto is an easy to use, lightning fast public transit navigation app featuring real-time tracking, live route 
disruption alerts, and an ad-free interface. Notable features include:

- **100% ad-free core experience**: pure focus on utility, performance and clean design.
- **Live transit map with real-time tracking**: open GTFS realtime integration to track buses and trains.
- **Departure subscriptions**: smart notifications that calculate live vehicle location + user walk time to alert users 
exactly when to head out the door.
- **Proactive service alert notifications**: instant warnings for line breakdowns or major delays before stepping into the 
station.

## Objectives and Success Metrics

> How do you know you've solved the problem? Whether it's increasing sign-ups, reducing churn, or whatever metrics are 
> most important to your business, you need to identify two or three clear goals with measurable outcomes that define what
> success looks like. These metrics will help you track progress and determine if your product is actually meeting its 
> goals.

1. **Daily Active User (DAU) Retention**: Achieve at least 35% 30-day retention rate within 6 months of launch, proving high
product utility and trust.
2. **On Time Catch Rate**: At least 90% of departure subscription alerts result in successful commuter boardings without 
missing the vehicle or spending >10 minutes waiting at the stop/station.
3. **Paid users conversion rate**: Achieve at least 3% conversion rate from active free users to paid tier.

## User Personas

> Who are you building this for? This is just a sentence or two detailing your ideal user, ensuring that you're focused 
> on real user needs. Understanding your target audience is crucial for creating a product that people will actually want
> to use.

1. **Jasmine**: As a 24 years old professional living in Petaling Jaya, she takes the Kelana Jaya LRT and RapidKL feeder 
bus to work daily. She demand zero clutter, accurate live tracking to optimize her morning routine down to the minute, 
and quick alerts when train lines fail. She also uses public transit during weekend outings.
2. **John**: As a traveler, he needs a clear, multi-modal routing across MRT, LRT and KTM without 
downloading multiple operator-specific apps or suffering through intrusive ads.
